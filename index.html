04 / TOR BRIDGES

When Tor is blocked,
try a bridge.

Bridges are unlisted Tor entry points. Get bridge lines from the Tor Project, then add them to Mulvakun's “Tor bridges (one per line)” setting.

A

Tor Project website

Open Advanced Options, choose an available transport such as obfs4 or webtunnel, complete the verification and copy the lines.

Get bridges ↗
B

Telegram bot

Open @GetBridgesBot and follow the bot's current instructions to request bridges.

Open bot ↗
C

Email

Ask bridges@torproject.org for a bridge type supported by your network, following the Tor Project's published guidance.

Write an email ↗
i

Add bridge lines: open Settings in Mulvakun, paste each line exactly as received, save, select Tor and connect. Leave the field empty for a direct Tor connection. Transport availability can vary by network and application version.
